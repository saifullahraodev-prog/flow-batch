## Leaving port
Welcome aboard. Today we follow a small cargo ship across the ocean. The crew loads hundreds of containers before sunrise. At six o'clock the captain sounds the horn, and the ship slowly leaves the harbour.

## The storm
At noon the sea is calm, and the captain checks the map. Suddenly, dark clouds appear on the horizon. The waves grow taller, but the ship holds steady.

## Safe arrival
By evening the storm has passed. The crew cheers as the lights of the port come into sight. Another voyage is complete. Thanks for watching!
